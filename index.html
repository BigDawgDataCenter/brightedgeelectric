Questions

Good to know

Are estimates really free?

Yes. Tell us about your project (photos help) and you’ll get a clear, free estimate.

What are your hours?

We’re available Monday through Sunday, 8 AM to 8 PM.

Do you offer emergency service?

Not at this time. For a power outage, contact your utility; if there’s smoke, sparks or any immediate danger, call 911 first.

Are you licensed?

Yes. Bright Edge Electric LLC is a licensed Washington electrical contractor (WA L&I license #BRIGHEE741DC), and the owner is a licensed master electrician. You can check any contractor’s license on the WA L&I Verify tool. (add: liability insurance amount)

Do you work on homes and businesses?

Both: single-family homes and small commercial businesses across King and Snohomish County.

What’s a “service upgrade”?

It means increasing the electrical capacity supplying power to your building, often needed when adding large new loads.
