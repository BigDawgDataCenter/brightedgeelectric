Privacy Policy

Updated: October 2026

This privacy policy explains how Bright Edge Electric LLC collects and uses your information.

Information We Collect

When you use our request form, we collect the following information:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • City or ZIP code
  • Home or business, the type of work, project details and timeline
  • How you prefer to be contacted
  • How you heard about us
  • Optional photos of your project

How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used only to respond to your request and to perform the job. We never sell or share your information for marketing purposes.

Photos and Location Data

When you submit optional photos through our request form, any location or camera data (EXIF data) is automatically removed upon receipt.

Storage and Security

Your requests are stored securely by our web provider, Big Dawg Data Center. Only the owner of Bright Edge Electric can see these requests; our web provider can access them only to keep the system running.

Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses no advertising or tracking cookies. We do load Google Fonts from Google to display our website properly, and like most websites, our hosting providers may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security.

Your Rights

You can ask us to see the information we have about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing or calling us.
